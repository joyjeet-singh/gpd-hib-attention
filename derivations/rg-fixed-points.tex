\section{MPO Formalism and RG Fixed-Point Equations}

We represent the attention operator $\hat{A}$ as a Matrix Product Operator (MPO). Given the sequence indices $i, j \in \{1, \dots, n\}$, the attention matrix $A_{ij}$ is expressed as:
\begin{equation}
    A_{ij} = \text{Tr} \left( M^{(1)}_{i_1 j_1} M^{(2)}_{i_2 j_2} \dots M^{(L)}_{i_L j_L} \right)
\end{equation}
where $L = \log_k n$ is the number of scales in a $k$-ary tree decomposition.

\subsection{RG Flow and Coarse-Graining}

We define the renormalization group (RG) flow using a MERA-like structure. Let $\mathcal{U}$ be the set of isometries $W$ and disentanglers $U$ that define the coarse-graining step. The attention operator at scale $s+1$ is related to the operator at scale $s$ by:
\begin{equation}
    \hat{A}^{(s+1)} = \mathcal{R}[\hat{A}^{(s)}] = \text{Tr}_{\text{fine}} \left( \mathcal{U}^{(s)} \hat{A}^{(s)} \mathcal{U}^{(s)\dagger} \right)
\end{equation}
where $\text{Tr}_{\text{fine}}$ denotes the partial trace over the degrees of freedom removed during coarse-graining.

\subsection{Variational Free Energy Minimization}

The fixed-point equations are derived by minimizing the variational free energy:
\begin{equation}
    F[\mathcal{U}] = \langle \hat{H} \rangle_{\hat{A}} - T S[\hat{A}]
\end{equation}
where $\hat{H}$ is the attention energy operator ($\hat{H} = -\beta^{-1} \ln \hat{A}$) and $S$ is the entanglement entropy.

At the fixed point, the isometry $W^*$ must satisfy the stationarity condition $\frac{\delta F}{\delta W} = 0$, leading to:
\begin{equation}
    W^* = \text{SVU} \left( \mathcal{E}(W^*) \right)
\end{equation}
where $\text{SVU}$ denotes the Procrustes projection (isometry from SVD) and $\mathcal{E}(W)$ is the environment of $W$ in the tensor network.

For the attention energy $E_{ij} = -q_i \cdot k_j$, the fixed-point operator $\hat{O}^*$ satisfies:
\begin{equation}
    \hat{O}^* = \sum_{\alpha} \lambda_{\alpha} \hat{V}_{\alpha} \otimes \hat{V}_{\alpha}^\dagger
\end{equation}
where $\hat{V}_{\alpha}$ are the eigen-operators of the RG flow, consistent with 1D quantum chain results where the fixed-point tensors encode the conformal data of the system.

\subsection{Verification}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Dimensional Consistency:} The fixed-point equations involve isometries $W$ which are dimensionless. The free energy $F$ has dimensions of energy (dimensionless in natural units). All terms in the variational update $W \propto \mathcal{E}(W)$ are consistent.
    \item \textbf{Energy Scale Preservation:} The isometries $W$ preserve the norm of the operators, ensuring that the attention energy scale $-q \cdot k$ is correctly mapped across scales without divergent scaling, provided $\beta$ is correctly renormalized.
    \item \textbf{Reduction to 1D Quantum Chain:} In the limit where the attention logits correspond to a local Hamiltonian (e.g., nearest-neighbor dot products), the MPO-RG flow reduces to the standard MERA flow for 1D quantum systems, recovering the expected critical exponents.
\end{enumerate}
