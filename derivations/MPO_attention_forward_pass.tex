\section{MPO-Attention Forward Pass Derivation}

The scaled dot-product attention is given by:
\[
A_{ij} = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{Q_i K_j^T}{\sqrt{d}}\right)V_j
\]
where $Q, K, V \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d}$.

\subsection{Hierarchical MERA-like Contraction}
We approximate the attention matrix $A$ as an MPO with bond dimension $\chi$.
For a sequence of length $n$, the MERA-like hierarchy contracts pairs of tokens:
\[
M^{(l)} = M_{left}^{(l)} \otimes M_{right}^{(l)}
\]
where $l$ is the layer index.

\subsection{Bias Support}
Bias matrices $B \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ are incorporated by augmenting the MPO cores:
\[
\tilde{M}^{(l)} = \begin{pmatrix} M^{(l)} & B^{(l)} \\ 0 & I \end{pmatrix}
\]
where $\text{rank}(B)=r$, making the augmented bond dimension $\chi+r$.
